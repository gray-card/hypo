# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Keeps exposure controls and logging within reach, with frame details and roll dates available on demand.
- Preserves frame drafts through account refreshes, supports recording past exposures, and exports roll exposures as CSV.
- Adds searchable offline development recipes, favorites, editable device recipes, and a dilution calculator.
- Keeps timer actions visible during development and supports elapsed time, Fahrenheit, and manual handoff between timed stages.
- Reduces timer persistence and widget updates to meaningful state changes.
- Opens galleries, scanning, workflows, following, and discovery through Hypo Web in the Library workspace.
- Updates Panproto Swift to 0.78.0.

- Initial native companion release.
- Organizes the app around four primary workspaces: Meter, Sessions, Rolls, and Library.
- Moves frame logging and film development into Sessions, with active development kept prominent.
- Gives physical rolls a direct home for finding a roll and checking its processing state.
- Groups reusable equipment, film reserves, chemistry, and development recipes in Library, with equipment first.
- Moves account and app settings into a global toolbar action.
- Preserves existing meter, frame-logging, development-timer, offline-sync, and deep-link workflows under the new hierarchy.
