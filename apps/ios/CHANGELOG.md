# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial native companion release.
- Organizes the app around four primary workspaces: Meter, Sessions, Rolls, and Library.
- Moves frame logging and film development into Sessions, with active development kept prominent.
- Gives physical rolls a direct home for finding a roll and checking its processing state.
- Groups reusable equipment, film reserves, chemistry, and development recipes in Library, with equipment first.
- Moves account and app settings into a global toolbar action.
- Preserves existing meter, frame-logging, development-timer, offline-sync, and deep-link workflows under the new hierarchy.
