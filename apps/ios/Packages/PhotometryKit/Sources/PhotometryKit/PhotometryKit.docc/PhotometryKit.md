# `PhotometryKit`

Perform exposure calculations with typed photographic quantities.

## Overview

PhotometryKit expresses sensitivity, aperture, time, exposure value, illuminance, luminance, stops, filters, reciprocity, flash, cine shutter, and Zone System placement without camera-framework dependencies. Initializers reject nonphysical values at the boundary.
